02

The data landscape, and what it forces

Four properties of this data are not cosmetic — each one rules a modeling decision in or out. These are computed directly from the files, not the blog.

41 / 4,905

The target matrix is extremely sparse. 3,596 compounds carry a label for exactly one isoform; only 41 are dense across all four. Four independent single-task models — the tutorial baseline — cannot borrow the CYP3A4-rich signal (47.6% coverage) to help sparse CYP2C9 (26.2%). Multitask sharing is not a nicety here; it is the main lever.

9.96×

Credible intervals are ~10× wider at low activity (median width 2.141 below pIC50 4 vs 0.215 above pIC50 5). This is the entire rationale for the ST-RAE metric — predictions inside the interval score zero error — and it means the right target is not a point estimate but a calibrated interval. The CI bounds ship in the training file, so you can train against true ST-RAE directly.

ρ up to 0.69

Isoforms are correlated but not redundant. CYP3A4↔CYP2C9 share the most (Spearman 0.69); CYP2D6 is the outlier (measured by a different assay — Echo-MS parent depletion, not fluorescence). Enough shared structure to justify multitask, enough divergence that a 2D6-specific treatment pays off.

|ρ| = 0.94

The primary screen is a real predictor, not just a hit filter. Single-concentration log2fc tracks DRC pIC50 at Spearman −0.83 to −0.94 across isoforms (−0.94 for CYP3A4). With 4,376 compounds — 3.6× the DRC set — this is a large, cheap auxiliary signal for pretraining or as a feature. Almost nobody will use it.

03

The TDI track is a shift-regression problem wearing a classifier's clothes

The is_TDI boolean is derived from two continuous pIC50 arms you already have. Learning the boolean directly throws that structure away.

Δ 0.53 vs 0.18

Median TDI shift is +0.53 log units for CYP3A4 positives vs +0.18 for negatives (2D6: +0.52 vs +0.11). The class boundary is literally the 0.301 (2-fold) shift line. Regress the shift, threshold it, tune the cut for MCC — this respects the label mechanism and lets low-activity "inferred positives" fall out of the same model.

84 & 51

Inferred positives are a non-trivial slice you must reconstruct correctly. Of 764 CYP3A4 positives, 680 are directly measured and 84 are inferred (low direct activity, measurable TDI arm); 2D6 is 273 + 51. Get the pIC50 4 / 4.301 logic wrong and you mislabel ~11% of the positive class before training starts.

04

Mechanism leaves fingerprints — and they differ by isoform

Time-dependent inhibition is mechanism-based: the enzyme turns the compound into a reactive species that disables it. So reactive-metabolite substructures should be enriched in TDI positives — and they are, isoform-specifically.

9.2×

Terminal alkynes are 9.2× enriched in CYP3A4 time-dependent inhibitors — a textbook mechanism-based inactivator motif (oxidation to a ketene/oxirene that adducts the heme). For CYP2D6 the driver is different: furans 2.5× and thiophenes 1.6×, while alkynes are actually depleted. A per-isoform, alert-aware model has a legible chemical rationale most black-box entries won't.

The routes — leaderboard, innovation, and science

Six routes, each anchored to a finding above. Leaderboard routes (teal) buy rank; innovation routes (amber) target the Most-Innovative-ML award, which is judged decoupled from rank; scientific routes (violet) produce reusable assets and open-code kudos.

ADIRECT INHIBITION · REGRESSION · MA-ST-RAE

Series-aware multitask baseline

Justified by 41/4,905 density + hit-expansion test structure. Random-split CV overstates skill; single-task wastes cross-isoform signal.

  • Cluster by Butina/Murcko scaffold → GroupKFold so analog series never split folds
  • Multitask over 4 isoforms (shared-trunk GBM stack or multitask chemprop GNN)
  • Auxiliary heads: single-conc log2fc + TDI-arm pIC50
  • Evaluate with the real ST-RAE using shipped CI bounds
effort mediumpayoff highinterim yes
BTDI · CLASSIFICATION · MCC · 3A4+2D6

Shift-regression → thresholded classifier

Justified by Δ 0.53 vs 0.18 separation and the 84/51 inferred positives. The boolean is a derived quantity — model the quantity.

  • Regress Δ = pIC50(+NADPH) − pIC50(direct); apply the 4 / 4.301 rules to label
  • Tune the decision threshold on MCC, per isoform
  • Borrow Emax all-isoform is_TDI as auxiliary supervision
  • Reconstruct labels once, unit-test them against the shipped booleans
effort mediumpayoff highinterim yes
CINNOVATION AWARD · UNCERTAINTY

Interval-native, metric-aligned learning

Justified by the ~10× CI fan-out. The metric rewards landing inside the credible interval — so predict the interval, not a point.

  • Heteroscedastic / quantile / evidential head predicting a per-compound distribution
  • Loss = differentiable ST-RAE surrogate against shipped CI bounds
  • Conformal calibration for guaranteed coverage on the blinded set
  • Rubric bullseye: "novel uncertainty-quantification strategies"
effort med-highpayoff award + rankinterim partial
DINNOVATION AWARD · INTERPRETABILITY

Mechanism-aware TDI

Justified by alkyne 9.2× (3A4) vs furan 2.5× (2D6). Reactive-metabolite chemistry is real, isoform-specific, and legible.

  • Structural-alert + site-of-metabolism features feeding the shift model
  • Per-isoform reactive-fragment library; report enrichment as evidence
  • Interpretable attribution tying predictions to mechanism, not fingerprints
  • The most communicable story in the field — strong webinar material
